Medical education platform designed to enhance learning and assessment for educators. 
Project Description: 
EminenceAI provides a robust medical education platform where students can take exams in tutor-guided and 
timed modes. Faculty can generate unlimited questions and use the "Ask Eminence" feature for complex 
queries. The RAG model allows deans to verify question quality and relevance. Administrators manage 
institutional subscriptions and verify subjects and faculty. This system supports continuous learning and 
assessment, empowering both educators and students in the medical field.
